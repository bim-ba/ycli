A series of questions combines multiple questions of other types. As users fill out your form, they can copy a series of questions and answer them again. This is convenient if they need to enter the same information multiple times in the same form.

For example, you can add a series of questions with the **First name**, **Last name**, and **Email** fields to a form for registering event participants. Then a user can create a group application for taking part in the event by copying the series of questions and entering multiple participants' information in the same form.

You can also use a series to group multiple questions together and set up common [display conditions](https://yandex.ru/support/forms/en/add-questions#conditions) for them.

To create a series of questions:

1.  In the list of questions, select **Question series**.

2.  Enter a name and ID for the series.

3.  To add a question to an empty series:

    -   Select a new question from the list within the **Question series** section.
    -   Alternatively, drag the icon of a previously created question onto the section.
4.  To add more questions:

    -   Hover over the top or bottom of the first question and click .
    -   Alternatively, drag the icon of a previously created question onto the **Question series** section.

You can [edit](https://yandex.ru/support/forms/en/add-questions#params) questions in a series the same way you edit separate questions.

-   To change where a series of questions is placed in your form, hover over the series and drag the icon. You can similarly rearrange questions inside a series.

-   To show or hide a series of questions based on previous answers, click **Display conditions** in the upper-right corner of the section. For more information, see [Configure display conditions for questions or pages](https://yandex.ru/support/forms/en/add-questions#conditions).
