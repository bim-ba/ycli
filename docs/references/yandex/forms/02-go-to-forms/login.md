1.  Go to the [https://forms.yandex.com/cloud/admin](https://forms.yandex.com/cloud/admin) page.

2.  Log in to the account that you use to work in the Yandex 360 for Business organization:

    -   To log in using your Yandex email address or an email address on your organization's domain, click **Log in to your Yandex account** and enter your login in one of the following formats: `login@yandex.com` or `login@example.com`.

    -   If organization uses the Single Sign-On authentication scheme with an identity federation, click **Log in via SSO**, specify the federation ID, and log in to your corporate account.
        Get your federation ID from your administrator.

        After that, the Yandex Forms home page opens.

3.  If you work with multiple organizations, click your avatar in the sidebar and make sure the appropriate organization is selected.

Organization employees may have the personal version of Yandex Forms selected. To switch between different Yandex Forms versions, select **Personal forms** at the top.
